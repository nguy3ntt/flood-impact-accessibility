# Dataset registry and M1 feasibility

M1 audited 2026-10-01; M2 extended the source inventory on 2026-10-02. The local source set is 27,077,879 bytes across 19 objects, including a 763,739-byte Spanish census-section pilot added for M2. This remains below the original 250 MiB pilot ceiling. Raw bytes, exact URLs, retrieval times, object generations where available, SHA-256 hashes and rights status are in the ignored `.project/m1_source_inventory.json`; data is never included in Git. The audit is a feasibility result, not a model or road-closure result. The canonical outputs are described in the [data contract](DATA_CONTRACT.md) and [reproducibility guide](REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

## Pilot observations that affect the protocol

- The three 512 × 512 raster trios align on EPSG:4326 grids. Labels actually contain `-1` (unknown), `0` (non-water) and `1` (water); the label GeoTIFF does **not** declare GDAL nodata. S1 contains VV/VH float32 dB with NaN nodata. S2 contains 13 described bands, stored as **int16** with zero nodata, despite upstream prose describing UInt16. The optical source has no packaged cloud QA band. M2 must inspect masks explicitly and must not treat unknown as dry.
- Pilot S1/S2 dates are the same day for Bolivia and one day apart for Ghana and Spain. A daily date alone cannot establish exact acquisition-hour coincidence. Clouds and changing flood conditions can invalidate a naive fusion target.
- The official hand-labelled files contain 252 train, 89 validation, 90 test and 15 separate Bolivia rows. The same ten named events appear in train, validation and test. These are chip-level splits suitable for a benchmark comparison, **not** evidence of generalisation to unseen events. M2 froze an [event-group split](../configs/event_split_v1.json) after auditing all 446 catalog footprints. Nigeria and Somalia are untouched final-test groups; Spain is exploratory because its pilot label was inspected during feasibility.
- The Spain pilot `Spain_7370579` intersects the AOI07 Copernicus flood-trace geometry at pixel level (97,267 raster cells at the pilot grid). Its label and Copernicus trace disagree in places because their dates, sensors and class meanings differ. No performance score is inferred from this check.

## Case-study decision

**Conditional Spain pilot:** use the 2019 Orihuela/Dolores area for the first geographically aligned exposure study, subject to M2 exact footprint/time checks and an M6 network reaching hospitals outside the Copernicus AOIs. EMSR388 is independent imagery interpretation, but its trace and damage classes do not validate safe travel or actual road closure. If a navigable wider graph cannot be assembled, narrow the case study to exposure and keep hospital accessibility explicitly scenario-only or choose another event before M6.

**Brisbane 2022 peak fusion: no-go with current evidence.** Council flood extent, roads, hospitals and origin geometry are accessible, but a [Brisbane flood study](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/14/19/5009) reports no Sentinel-1 overpass at the flood peak, while nearby HLS optical listings are cloud affected. The 2022 extent and current infrastructure can still support a clearly dated scenario or transfer illustration; they cannot be labelled as validated peak-time Sen1Floods11 fusion or verified closure evidence. Reconsider only if event-aligned radar/optical observations and independent reference evidence are demonstrated.

## Acquisition order after M1

1. M2: stage only needed event chips, source metadata and small case vectors; confirm event aliases, footprints, dates and valid masks.
2. Build canonical raster/vector derivatives under ignored roots with source and environment hashes.
3. Promote to larger benchmark subsets only after storage and overlap audits. The three pilot trios total 9.49 MB; multiplying their average by 446 hand-labelled rows suggests roughly 1.4 GB of raw trios, **only a rough extrapolation**, before derivatives, caches or checkpoints. The observed free E: space after the pilot is about 4.77 GiB, so staged acquisition is required.
4. Defer weak labels, full archive and foundation weights until their experiment gates and rights/capacity reviews.
