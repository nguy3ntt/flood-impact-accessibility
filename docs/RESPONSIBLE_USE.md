# Interpretation and limitations

This is retrospective research/scenario analysis. It does not certify safe travel, establish real-time closure, recommend evacuation routes or measure water depth. Roads may be impassable without mapped water, or pass above water on bridges. Clouds, radar artefacts, acquisition delays, permanent water and incomplete networks affect interpretation.

Always show source dates, footprint, unknown areas, graph assumptions, model version and scenario policy. Describe changes in simulated reachability and estimated cost, not proven community isolation. Do not equate hospitals mapped in a dataset with available care, capacity or operational status.

Modern roads/facilities used over historical imagery introduce temporal mismatch. Current or absent population data limits population-impact claims. GeoAI pretraining may overlap evaluation regions; disclose available pretraining provenance and avoid overstating pristine zero-shot novelty.

Scenario probabilities, calibration and uncertainty claims require evidence appropriate to their unit. Pixel confidence is not road passability confidence. Synthetic demo values remain explicitly synthetic in CLI/UI/export metadata.
